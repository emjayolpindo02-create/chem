Golden Gas Law Rules

  • Temperature must ALWAYS be in Kelvin: K = °C + 273.15
  • Pressure unit consistency: 1 atm = 760 mmHg = 760 torr = 101.325 kPa
  • Standard Temperature & Pressure (STP): 0°C (273.15 K) and 1 atm (101.3 kPa)
  • Standard Molar Volume at STP: 1 mole = 22.414 L
